# Roadmap

## 0.2 — stronger provenance and evaluation

- optional signed source and approval receipts;
- externally anchored checkpoints and trusted-clock profiles;
- W3C PROV export;
- richer forecast calibration and scoring rules;
- sensitivity analysis for criteria, scenario, and robust weights;
- value-of-information and option-discovery diagnostics;
- reviewer disagreement and adjudication records;
- private-case redaction and synthetic analogue tooling;
- Windows and macOS filesystem/symlink CI;
- signed release attestations when an appropriate identity is available.

## Non-goals

- automatic external execution;
- retroactive rewriting of historical decisions;
- legal, medical, financial, or production certification;
- public storage of private evidence or personal profiles.
