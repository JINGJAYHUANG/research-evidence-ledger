# Glossary

- **Cutoff:** latest time information may enter a decision snapshot.
- **Vintage:** one version of a source or assumption lineage.
- **Freeze:** deterministic construction of the knowable information set.
- **Replay:** application of the locked rule to a frozen snapshot.
- **Regret:** gap from the best option under a scenario or realized outcome.
- **Hard gate:** non-compensable integrity or eligibility requirement.
- **Prospective rule change:** a change that applies only to future decisions.
- **Decision-quality quadrant:** joint label for process integrity and outcome.
- **Merkle checkpoint:** compact digest of audit-record hashes; unsigned here.
