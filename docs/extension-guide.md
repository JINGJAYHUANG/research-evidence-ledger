# Extension guide

A new case should include:

1. at least two options, including a status quo;
2. at least one reversible option;
3. two or more evidence families for high-consequence confirmed claims;
4. assumptions with ranges, falsifiers, and monitor owners;
5. scenarios whose probabilities sum to one;
6. versioned assessments covering every option and scenario;
7. a locked rule with normalized criteria and robust weights;
8. explicit approvals and action state;
9. later outcomes and a review;
10. a public-boundary statement.

A real private case should remain outside the public repository. Export only a
sanitized synthetic analogue.
