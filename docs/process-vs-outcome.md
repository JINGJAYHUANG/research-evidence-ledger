# Process quality versus outcome

A decision can be well made and still lose. It can also be badly made and win.
The review therefore reports both:

- process score and failed hard gates;
- realized utility and ex-post regret.

The four quadrants are descriptive labels for the synthetic record. They do not
estimate counterfactual welfare in a real organization.

A favorable outcome must not erase future leakage, approval bypass, or other
process failures. An unfavorable outcome must not retroactively introduce
information that was unavailable at the decision cutoff.
